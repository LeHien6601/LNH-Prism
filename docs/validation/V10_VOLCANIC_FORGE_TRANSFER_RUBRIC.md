# V10 Volcanic Forge Style-Transfer Review Rubric

## Status

**Approved review instrument.** M10-A3 accepted Option B on 2026-07-19 with explicit lava-and-ember emission limits. It becomes actionable only at M10-A7 after the M10-A6 evidence package is complete.

## Technical hard preflight

Before scoring, confirm deterministic reproduction, stable IDs, complete source/material/font provenance, engine-neutral modular delivery, editable SVG layers, recorded seeds and zero baseline, valid manifests/receipts, clean reproduction, no reference-pixel contamination, no Volcanic Forge renderer branch, valid functional bounds, and target-phone readability. Any failure blocks a pass regardless of score.

## Required review distances

| Distance | Required checks |
|---|---|
| Source scale | Shared edge/material/variation/ornament/focal/typography/lighting layers are independently inspectable and data-bound. |
| Target-phone scale | Text/state readability, molten focal clarity, restrained emission/embers, content bounds, progress visibility, and primary/secondary action hierarchy. |
| Thumbnail scale | Clear distinction from Frostbound in temperature, material language, focal treatment, silhouette, and composition path. |

## Automatic blockers

- A palette-only reskin or visual ambiguity with Frostbound at thumbnail scale.
- A Volcanic Forge-specific renderer branch or altered shared-template geometry.
- Missing provenance, manifests, receipts, recorded seeds, or engine-neutral module extraction.
- Reference pixels, flattened production sources, unseeded variation, broken clipping/content bounds, or unreadable action text.
- Lava, embers, ornaments, or glow obscuring the focal/action hierarchy.
- More than eight portrait-composition embers, lava emission opacity above `0.55`, glow radius above `12%` of the smaller component dimension, or any ember emission from an individual control.

## Pass criteria

A pass requires every hard-preflight item, a recorded score of at least `85/100`, no dimension below `3/5`, and no automatic blocker. The record must name the reviewer, evidence paths, each score, observations, blockers, comparison result, and final decision.

## Required comparison finding

The review must explicitly state why Volcanic Forge is not Frostbound with warmer colors: evidence must show materially different surface response, lighting direction, ornament grammar, variation, typography, focal treatment, and state behavior while retaining the same generalized renderer systems.
