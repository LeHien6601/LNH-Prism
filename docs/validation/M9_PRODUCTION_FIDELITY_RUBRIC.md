# M9 Production-Fidelity Review Rubric

## Status

Draft review instrument. It becomes actionable only at M9-A11 after the M9-A10 evidence package is complete.

## Hard preflight

The reviewer must confirm deterministic reproduction, stable IDs, full provenance and receipts, engine-neutral modular delivery, editable structure, recorded seeds, no reference-pixel contamination, no broken functional bounds, and target-phone readability. Any failure blocks a pass regardless of score.

## Three required review distances

| Distance | Required checks |
|---|---|
| Source scale | Edge stack order, material channels, clipping, facets, variation masks, and layer isolation. |
| Target-phone scale | Text/state readability, focal clarity, border density, glow restraint, content bounds, and action hierarchy. |
| Thumbnail scale | Silhouette, focal dominance, primary/secondary action separation, panel-pattern restraint, and composition path. |

## Visual score — separate from technical correctness

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Silhouette and angular-language fidelity | 10 |
| Material separation and response depth | 15 |
| Edge depth and bevel hierarchy | 15 |
| Shared-lighting coherence | 10 |
| Focal-object strength and hierarchy | 15 |
| Ornament placement and structural restraint | 10 |
| Controlled seeded variation | 10 |
| Composition and visual hierarchy | 10 |
| Mobile-scale readability | 5 |
| **Total** | **100** |

Technical correctness is a gate, not a score multiplier. A technically valid package can still fail visual review. The review record must identify the reviewer, evidence addresses, individual scores, blockers, observations, and the decision; agents may prepare but not assign the final score.

## Pass criteria

A pass requires every hard-preflight item, a human-recorded score of at least `85/100`, no dimension below `3/5`, and no automatic blocker. `V8-O001` must be specifically assessed at thumbnail and target-phone scale: the crystal focal must retain dominance while panel pattern remains supporting detail.
